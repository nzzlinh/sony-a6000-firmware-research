This repository is an independent research project about the Sony ILCE-6000
(α6000). It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Sony. “Sony”,
“α6000”, “ILCE-6000”, and related names and logos remain the property of their
respective owners and are used only to identify the research subject.

## Sony firmware and derived material

Sony firmware, updater packages, decrypted firmware trees, camera dumps, Sony
source code, and personal camera data are not distributed here. The repository
contains hashes, inventories, facts, limited metadata, and human-written
research notes derived from privately obtained artifacts.

The metadata-only records under `analysis/evidence/` intentionally exclude
decompiler pseudocode, assembly listings, strings, reference graphs, and native
value-flow data. The full REA outputs remain in private research storage. No
license is granted to Sony firmware or to Sony-derived material by the project
licenses.

inspect. Do not redistribute proprietary firmware, credentials, private dumps,
